# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Two primary users, served equally:
- **Small-business owners** in Brazil (restaurants, bars, shops, service providers, creators) editing their own link-in-bio page, often alone and often on the phone, with no design background.
- **Agencies and social-media managers** who manage the pages of several clients from one account, mostly on a computer, and need speed and organization.

## Product Purpose
Virtú.bio (vmbio.pro) lets a business publish a link-in-bio page that looks like a professional mini-site, edit it without design skills, and see who visits and what they click. Success: the owner finds every function without help, the page looks distinct from generic link lists, and free users see enough value to upgrade.

## Positioning
- Premium designed models: pages that look like a real site, not a stack of identical buttons.
- Many clients in one account: agencies manage every client page in one place.
- Detailed statistics: visits, clicks per button, origin, device, city, UTM, live visitors.

## Operating Context
Owners share the page in their Instagram bio, WhatsApp and printed material. Agencies switch between clients during the day. The editor shows a live phone preview of the page while editing.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Plans: Grátis (basic, no card), Essencial R$ 9,90 (premium models, video, full statistics, unlimited short links), Profissional R$ 29,90 (everything in Essencial plus custom forms, verified badge, page without the Virtú.bio mark).
- The "Modelos" area is a sales showcase: it must make free users want the paid plan.
- "Formulários" lets the Profissional client build their own forms and see responses instead of using another tool.
- Clients' published pages and the dashboard sidebar have their own looks; dashboard redesigns must not change clients' published pages.
- Stack: Next.js 15, React 19, Tailwind v4, Supabase, Stripe.

## Brand Commitments
- Brazilian Portuguese UI; plain, direct language.
- The owner explicitly wants a look that is beautiful and sophisticated and does not read as AI-generated.
- Current type: Instrument Sans (text) and Bricolage Grotesque (titles); ink indigo #191A3D, brand blue #2440F0, light paper.
- The owner liked how the buttons appear in the editor's phone preview (white rounded buttons on the page's color); keep that.

## Evidence on Hand
Real example page: HORSE BBQ Smoke'n Grill (handle horsebbq). Real click counts from its Hopp page: Cardápio digital 2,3 mil, Faça sua reserva 451, Localização 331, Peça pelo iFood 280; 3.639 views. No testimonials or customer counts exist; do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Everything has one obvious place: content, appearance, models and settings never mixed.
2. Show the real page: previews and model thumbnails are the product, not placeholders.
3. Sell by showing: paid features appear in context with a clear lock and upgrade path, never as nagging.
4. Plain words over jargon: say what each thing does.
